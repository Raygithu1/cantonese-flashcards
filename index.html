How it works

Tap. Listen. Copy.

No lessons, no scores, no pressure. Just a grown-up and a little voice playing together.

  1. 1 Look at the big Chinese character and the cheerful picture.
  2. 2 Press the big button to hear it in Cantonese, as many times as you like.
  3. 3 Say it back using the Jyutping and “sounds like” hints.
